LOCATION

The Rath is strategically, pivotally and conveniently positioned, close to the Meath / Dublin border, essentially just north of Ashbourne, adjacent to where the M2 and N2 meet and to the Pillo Hotel.

Ashbourne, one of County Meath`s fastest-growing commuter towns, offers a vibrant mix of suburban convenience, strong community spirit, and modern family living. Located approximately 20km north of Dublin city centre along the M2 corridor, it is particularly popular with families and professionals due to its quick access to the M50, Dublin Airport, and regular scheduled Bus Éireann services into the capital via 103 route, amongst others.

Retail offerings include many outlets like those located at High Street Ashbourne and Ashbourne Retail Park as well as those along Ashbourne`s Main Street. Aldi, Choice, Dunnes Stores, Jysk, Lidl, Mr Price, SuperValu and Tesco Extra all have a presence in Ashbourne.

Schools include primary/national educational campuses at St. Mary`s, St. Declan`s, Ashbourne Educate Together and Ashbourne Community as well as Irish-medium, Gaelscoil na Mí. Post-primary/secondary options include those at Ashbourne Community School and Coláiste De Lacy.

Ashbourne scores well for its extensive sporting culture and recreational facilities. They include Donaghmore Ashbourne GAA Club, Ashbourne Golf Club and Ashbourne Rugby Club and Ashbourne United AFC as well as cricket and baseball clubs. Ashbourne Community Centre features multi-purpose sports halls, squash courts, handball alleys, pre-school space, and community meeting rooms. The Pillo Hotel provides full leisure club facilities, including a gym, swimming pool and spa. Vue have a multi-screen cinema based at Ashbourne Retail Park. Further family leisure and access to the great outdoors is available locally for exploration at Emerald Park, a popular theme park and zoo (formerly Tayto Park); Hill of Tara, Balrath Woods and the Brú na Bóinne complex of Neolithic tombs comprising Dowth, Knowth and Newgrange.
